## 4. Common Objections & Plain-English Answers

- *"Will it sound like a bot?"* -> Every draft reads from your `voice.md` file calibrated on five real examples of your own writing.
- *"What if it makes a mistake?"* -> External messages land in Drafts first; nothing sends without your click.
