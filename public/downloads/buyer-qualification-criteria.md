# Buyer Qualification & Offer Criteria Sheet
# Agentic Architect · https://agenticarchitect.ai/starter-kit

## 1. Ideal Client & Decision-Maker Profile
- **Primary Economic Buyer:** [e.g., Founder, Managing Partner, COO, VP of Operations, or Department Head at a 5 to 250-person business]
- **Internal Operator / Champion:** [e.g., Executive Assistant, Operations Manager, Chief of Staff, or Technical Lead running day-to-day workflows]
- **Existing Workspace Stack:** [e.g., Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, HubSpot/Salesforce, Slack, Cal.com, QuickBooks/Stripe]

## 2. Top 3 Urgent Bottlenecks That Trigger Immediate Action
1. **Slow Lead Response & Manual Pre-Call Research:** Prospects wait hours for replies while senior staff spend 15 to 20 minutes researching every call by hand.
2. **Post-Call Proposal & Onboarding Drag:** Discovery calls stall for days waiting on manual scope writeups, contract prep, and kickoff checklists.
3. **High-Margin Capacity Lost to Admin Triage:** Founders and senior operators lose 8 to 15 hours per week to inbox sorting, scheduling, and recurring status rollups.

## 3. Green-Flag Qualification Signals (Route to Priority Call)
- Team already runs a repeatable sales or delivery process with 10+ weekly inquiries or recurring client touchpoints.
- Clear economic owner with urgency to reclaim weekly capacity or avoid a $65,000+ administrative hire.
- Values human-reviewed accuracy, auditability, and owning workflows inside their own workspace accounts.

## 4. Red-Flag Disqualifiers (Route to Self-Serve Resources)
- Looking for spammy mass cold outreach or unreviewed automated external messaging.
- Pre-revenue idea stage with no repeatable customer or operational workflow yet.
- Unwilling to assign one internal point of contact to review staged drafts.
