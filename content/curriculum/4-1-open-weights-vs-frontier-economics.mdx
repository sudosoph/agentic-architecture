## Memory Bandwidth Is Destiny: The Only Formula Hardware Buyers Need

Autoregressive token generation at low concurrency is **memory-bandwidth bound**, not FLOP bound. For every generated token, the GPU or unified-memory bus must read all **active** weights out of RAM:

```text
Max Single-Stream tok/s ≈ (Effective Memory Bandwidth in GB/s) / (Active Model Footprint in GB)
```

That single equation explains why **Mixture-of-Experts (`MoE`)** architectures transformed private enterprise compute in 2026:
- A dense `70B` model at 4-bit quantization reads `~38 GB` on *every* token (`~20 tok/s` on an `800 GB/s` bus).
- A sparse MoE model with `35B` total parameters and `3B` active parameters (or `Mistral Large 4` with `52B` active out of `1T` total on multi-node / high-RAM rigs) activates only a fraction of its weights per token, delivering snappy interactive speeds once total weights fit cleanly inside unified memory or VRAM.

## When Private Compute Wins vs. When Zero-Retention APIs Win

Do not buy hardware out of ideology; buy it when physics, compliance, or utilization math favors ownership:

1. **Zero-Data-Retention (`ZDR`) Cloud APIs Win When:** Traffic is spiky, concurrency swings 20x during business hours, tasks require frontier Tier-1 synthesis (`GPT-6.2 Astra`, `Claude Fable 5.2`), and standard BAA/DPA terms satisfy legal.
2. **Dedicated / Private Open-Weight Rigs Win When:**
   - **Strict Zero-Egress Compliance:** Unredacted PHI, privileged M&A files, defense/IP codebases, or sovereign client mandates forbid third-party packet egress altogether.
   - **Sustained High-Duty-Cycle Loops:** Continuous background extraction, repo indexing, or test-verification loops running 8+ hours daily amortize a `$4,500 to $12,000` desk node in **4 to 9 months** compared against uncapped cloud seats and API bills.
   - **Hardened Local Harnesses:** Pairing `OpenCode` or internal services directly against local `vLLM` / `llama.cpp` with cloud fallbacks disabled in config guarantees zero accidental leakage.
