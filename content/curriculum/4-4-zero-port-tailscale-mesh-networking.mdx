## Never Port-Forward `8000` or `11434` to the Public Internet

Tens of thousands of self-hosted inference servers sit exposed on Shodan because teams opened router ports or bound inference servers to `0.0.0.0` without authentication.

With **Tailscale Serve** (built on WireGuard), your private inference node keeps **zero inbound WAN ports open**:

1. Bind `vLLM` or `llama-server` strictly to `127.0.0.1:8000`.
2. Publish it solely inside your encrypted tailnet with automatic TLS certificates (`tailscale serve --bg 8000`).
3. Enforce **Tailscale ACL tags** (`tag:ai-node`, `tag:ops-worker`, `tag:staff-laptop`) so inference nodes can accept port `443` requests from authorized workers while being blocked from initiating lateral connections back into employee machines.
