## Why 90% of "AI Marketing SaaS" Hurts Brand Equity

Buying a `$99/month` "1-click AI blog and LinkedIn generator" produces the exact median phrasing your buyers have trained their eyes to skip, and triggers anti-slop classifiers across search engines and newsletter networks.

## Marketing Build-vs-Buy Breakdown

- **Worth Buying / Connecting:** Primary search, SERP, and competitive intelligence data APIs (`Semrush`, `Ahrefs`, `Tavily` / `Exa` live research), design/video asset infrastructure where human art direction stays in control, and verified analytics/attribution (`PostHog`, `Umami`).
- **Skip & Build Bespoke In-House:** Generic "AI brand copywriters," automated social comment bots (which destroy executive trust in seconds), and rigid content-repurposing SaaS wrappers.

## Architecture of an In-House Interview-Driven Content Harness

1. **Signal Ingestion:** Pull real customer discovery questions from call transcripts, support tickets, and curated industry RSS/forum radars.
2. **Productive-Friction Author Interview (10 Mins):** Before drafting a word, an interview skill asks your subject-matter expert 4 sharp questions to extract one contrarian point of view, one real number or scar, and one concrete trade-off.
3. **Multi-Format Compilation + Human Polish:** Compile the long-form canonical essay, newsletter edition, and platform-native summaries against strict style rules (zero cliches, real citations), staging drafts for human cold-open writing and final approval.
