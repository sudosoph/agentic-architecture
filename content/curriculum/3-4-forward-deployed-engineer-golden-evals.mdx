## Why the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Model Won Enterprise AI

Traditional management consultancies spend eight weeks interviewing executives to deliver a 90-slide strategy deck that nobody in engineering can implement. Traditional IT queues ask business leaders to write rigid requirements up front for probabilistic workflows they have never seen run.

Pioneered by Palantir and adopted across top 2026 enterprise AI deployments (`Harvey`, `Sierra`, frontier lab enterprise teams), the **Forward Deployed AI Engineer (FDE)** collapses strategy and engineering into one person sitting directly beside the operator:

1. **Days 1 to 2 (Shadow & Extract):** Watch 5 real transactions live (not how the org chart claims they happen, how staff actually click through spreadsheets and emails). Run Socratic extraction to capture unwritten exceptions.
2. **Days 3 to 5 (Thin Vertical Slice on Real Data):** Wire a minimal end-to-end Level-2 Draft-First harness connected to real staging data so operators react to concrete outputs by Friday.
3. **Week 2 (Error Analysis & Golden Eval Lock-In):** Harvest every correction operators make on those first 25 runs and turn them into an automated regression gate.

## Building a 25-Case Binary Golden Eval Suite (Zero Guesswork)

Never write evaluation metrics before looking at data:

1. **Collect 25 real historical inputs** (15 routine, 10 gnarly edge cases).
2. **Open-code the failures:** Inspect outputs yourself and write plain notes (*"Missed renewal auto-escalation clause"*, *"Invented discount tier"*).
3. **Cluster into 3 to 5 Binary Pass/Fail Checks (`PASS` / `FAIL` only):**
   - Prefer **deterministic code checks** first (valid JSON schema, verbatim quote present in source text, zero prohibited phrases, math totals match).
   - Use a **narrow binary LLM-Judge** strictly for semantic checks (*"Did the draft ask all 3 required intake questions before proposing scope? YES/NO"*), calibrated against your own human labels until agreement exceeds `90%`.
