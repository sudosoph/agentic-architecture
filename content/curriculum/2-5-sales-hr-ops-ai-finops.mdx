## Sales, RevOps & People Ops: Automate the Prep and Hygiene, Never the Human Relationship

In Revenue and People Operations, the fastest way to damage brand reputation is letting an unreviewed bot blast prospects or send canned HR replies. Keep **100% of external relationship touchpoints human**, and automate the invisible 70% of administrative drag around them:

- **RevOps & Account Executives:** Calendar webhook -> live company and news research -> structured pre-call brief 30 minutes prior -> post-call transcript extraction staging CRM field updates, mutual action plans, and draft follow-up notes in under 60 seconds.
- **HR & Internal Operations:** Replace Slack shoulder-taps (*"What is our L&D policy?"*, *"How do we onboard a contractor?"*) with a citation-locked internal handbook connector that refuses to answer whenever the source doc is out of date, flagging the exact doc owner to update it.

## The AI FinOps Audit: Funding Transformation by Cutting Seat Sprawl

Organizations routinely discover they are paying for **four overlapping layers of AI markup**:
1. Workspace suite AI add-ons (`$20 to $30/user/mo`)
2. Standalone frontier chat seats (`$25 to $60/user/mo`)
3. Point-solution meeting-note, writing, and CRM summary wrappers (`$30 to $100/user/mo` each)
4. Uncapped developer API keys running Tier-1 frontier models on Tier-3 background jobs

Pull 90 days of SSO usage and invoice data, consolidate onto **one governed workspace anchor + one direct multi-model API gateway**, and reallocate the recovered 40% to 70% budget into high-leverage bespoke harnesses that your company permanently owns.
