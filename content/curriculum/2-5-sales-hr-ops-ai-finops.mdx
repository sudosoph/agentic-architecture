## Sales, RevOps & People Ops: Automate the Prep and Hygiene, Never the Human Relationship

In Revenue and People Operations, the fastest way to torch brand reputation is letting an unreviewed bot blast prospects or send canned HR brush-offs. Keep **100% of external relationship touchpoints human**, and automate the invisible 70% of administrative drag around them:

- **RevOps & Account Executives:** Calendar webhook -> live company + SEC/news research -> structured pre-call brief 30 minutes prior -> post-call transcript extraction staging CRM field updates, mutual action plans, and draft follow-up notes in under 60 seconds.
- **HR & Internal Operations:** Replace Slack shoulder-taps (*"What is our L&D stipend rule?"*, *"How do we onboard a contractor?"*) with a citation-locked internal handbook connector that refuses to answer whenever the source doc is out of date, flagging the exact doc owner to update it.
