## Why 75% of Enterprise Seat Rollouts Stall: "Productivity Hoarding"

Across mid-market and enterprise rollouts, leadership buys AI licenses, runs a 60-minute "Intro to Prompting" webinar, and sees flat P&L impact three months later.

Often, individual employees *are* saving four hours a week, **and deliberately hiding it**. When workers fear that admitting a task now takes 15 minutes instead of 4 hours will trigger headcount cuts or piled-on busywork, they keep their prompts private. Organizational capability stays at zero.

## Three Structural Fixes That Unlock Shared Adoption

1. **Reward Contributed `Skills`, Not Secret Time Saved:** Make promoting a tested workflow or `SKILL.md` into the team's shared repository a visible career and bonus multiplier, exactly like an engineer contributing reusable internal tooling.
2. **Guarantee Capacity Reinvestment:** State clearly from the CEO/COO seat what reclaimed hours fund: higher client touch, faster deal turnaround, eliminating weekend fire drills, or expanding revenue per team without burnout.
3. **Replace Generic Training With Functional Build Sprints:** Never put Legal, Finance, Marketing, and Engineering in the same generic "ChatGPT 101" lecture. Train shared core foundations (Modules 1.1 to 1.3) in 90 minutes, then split immediately into department-specific workflow labs where every participant ships one governed artifact on real work.

## The 90-Day AI Operating Cadence

- **Days 1 to 30 (Audit & Guardrails):** Publish the 1-page Green/Amber/Red data policy, select 1 flagship process per department, and run Socratic extraction interviews to document tribal rules.
- **Days 31 to 60 (Pilot & Golden Rubric):** Wire Level-2 Draft-First workflows in each department and test against 25 historical cases before broadening access.
- **Days 61 to 90 (Shared Skill Repo & FinOps Review):** Consolidate working `Skills` into a versioned company library, cancel redundant SaaS point tools, and track **cycle-time compression** (for example: *RFP turnaround cut from 4 days to 3 hours*) rather than self-reported time surveys.
