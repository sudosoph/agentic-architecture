## Legal AI's Real Value: Consistency Against Your Playbook, Not Freeform Lawyering

General counsel and commercial legal teams get burned when staff ask a generic chatbot *"Review this MSA for issues."* Without your company's exact fallback positions, liability caps, governing-law preferences, and IP ownership rules, a generic review flags harmless boilerplate while missing a toxic uncapped indemnification clause.

## Weighing `Harvey` & `Legora` Against Bespoke Playbook Harnesses

- **When `Harvey` or `Legora` Justifies Enterprise Spend:** Large law firms and global legal departments processing thousands of multi-jurisdiction matters that need bundled authoritative legal research corpora, Word add-in redlining UX, `ISO 42001` / `SOC2 Type II` vendor risk transfer, and embedded vendor deployment support.
- **When a Bespoke Clause Harness Wins at 1/10th the Cost:** In-house legal, procurement, and RevOps teams reviewing recurring NDAs, SaaS vendor DPAs, customer MSAs, and clinical/partner agreements against **15 to 40 well-defined company positions**.

## Designing a Zero-Hallucination Contract Triage Harness

1. **Privilege & Zero-Retention Boundary First:** Route strictly over a Zero-Data-Retention (`ZDR`) enterprise endpoint, 3P VPC gateway, or private open-weight node (`Mistral Large 4` / `Qwen 3.8` on private compute).
2. **Verbatim Span Citation Requirement:** Every flagged risk must return `{ clause_type, verbatim_quote, section_number, playbook_standard, severity, proposed_redline }`. A deterministic post-check verifies `verbatim_quote` exists character-for-character inside the source document; if not, the check fails loudly instead of passing a hallucinated quote to counsel.
3. **Productive-Friction Deal Context Check:** Before generating redlines, the harness prompts the deal owner for three facts: *Counterparty role (vendor vs. customer), annual contract value, and whether Red-tier PII/PHI is touched.*
