Most professionals still use frontier AI like a 2023 search bar: type twelve words, get generic corporate filler, spend twenty minutes rewriting it, and conclude AI is overrated.

High-output operators work completely differently:

### Post 2 · Department Build-vs-Buy Matrix
Every COO, CFO, and General Counsel is getting pitched dozens of per-seat vertical AI wrappers right now.

Before signing six disconnected tools that cannot share company context, run a three-lane check:
- Buy mature vertical platforms when they bring proprietary external data, payment rails, or deep regulatory transfer.
- Compose lightweight in-house workflows whenever a process spans your internal systems or encodes competitive judgment.

### Post 3 · Three-Tier Model Routing & Subscription Math
You do not need to route 100% of company traffic to your priciest flagship endpoint, and you do not need $200/month of overlapping AI wrappers.

Routing 80% of routine triage and JSON extraction to fast workhorse or permissive open-weight models while reserving deep reasoning for the hardest 5% cuts inference spend by 70% to 84% without hurting quality.
