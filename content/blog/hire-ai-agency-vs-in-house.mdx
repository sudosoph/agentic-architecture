Once a founder, COO, or VP of Operations decides *"We need real AI workflows running across our team this quarter, not just random ChatGPT seats,"* four options land on the table:

1. Tell everyone to watch YouTube tutorials and figure it out solo (**Unstructured DIY**).
2. Pay a traditional **AI Automation Agency** `$15,000 to $35,000+` on an open-ended retainer.
3. Train yourself or your EA/Ops Lead via a **Structured Workflow Course (`$297`) or Guided Cohort (from `$1,497`)**, or commission a **Focused 1-Bottleneck Sprint (from `$2,500`)** inside your own accounts.
4. Bring an experienced practitioner in for a **Private Corporate AI Enablement Workshop** for 10 to 100+ staff.

Having sat on the enterprise side at McKinsey (cutting $70M in SaaS OpEx and rolling out clinical software to 250+ healthcare leaders), in applied AI engineering, and inside classrooms with 300+ founders at Boulder Startup Week, here is the candid math on where each model wins and where buyers get burned.

## Why 70% of black-box agency retainers get switched off within 90 days

Business workflows evolve constantly: your pricing changes, an intake question gets updated, or you add a new service tier. When an outside contractor stitches twenty undocumented webhook nodes together behind their own agency credentials, nobody on your staff dares touch it. The first time an edge case fails on a Tuesday afternoon, your team reverts to manual spreadsheets.

Whether you learn in our courses or commission a focused custom sprint, durable automation follows two rules: keep company context in plain-English markdown files ([`voice.md`, `icp.md`](/starter-kit)) and ship clean, documented workflows directly inside *your* accounts so editing a rule takes three minutes.
