## Why 70% of "Done-For-You AI Agency" builds get turned off within 90 days

Business workflows are living organisms: your pricing changes, a CRM field gets renamed, or you launch a new service tier. When an outside agency stitches twenty undocumented webhook nodes together behind their own contractor credentials, nobody on your team dares touch it. The first time an edge case breaks on a Tuesday afternoon, your staff reverts to manual spreadsheets.

By contrast, **Done-With-You implementation** pairs portable plain-English context files ([`voice.md`, `icp.md`](/starter-kit)) with clean 4-step workflows you and your operations teammate wire inside your own accounts during live weekly sessions. When your business evolves next quarter, editing `voice.md` takes three minutes.
