Walk behind ten employees using ChatGPT or Claude inside an enterprise today and you will see the same failure pattern nine times out of ten.

Someone opens a blank chat window, types a twelve-word instruction (*"Draft our Q1 vendor renewal negotiation plan for Acme"* or *"Review this client SOW for risk"*), hits Enter, and receives eight hundred words of polished, confident prose that silently invents pricing thresholds, ignores legal fallback positions, and misses the single operational constraint that actually mattered.

Teams call this a "model hallucination problem" and wait for the next frontier release to fix it. Even with late-2026 frontier models (`GPT-6.2 Astra`, `Claude Fable 5.2`, `Gemini 4 Carbon`), **no amount of pre-training teaches a model unwritten rules that exist only inside your CFO's or General Counsel's head.**

## Zero Intake Friction Manufactures Guesswork

Instruction-tuned models optimize heavily for immediate helpfulness. When you supply 15% of the required decision context on Turn 1, a zero-friction chat interface forces the model to hallucinate the missing 85% from internet medians.

The fix is not "longer prompt templates" pasted from a cheat sheet. The fix is **Productive Friction**: engineering AI harnesses and reusable skills that **refuse to draft prematurely and instead interview, prod, and challenge the human operator first**.

## Three Mechanisms Powering Interview-First AI in Late 2026

Three ecosystem shifts converged this year to make Interview-First systems practical for non-engineers and engineering teams alike:

1. **Unified Workspace & Local Folder Execution:** With Anthropic unifying Chat and **Cowork** inside Claude Enterprise (combining sandbox execution, local project folder mounts, and admin-governed `.mcpb` Desktop Extensions) alongside terminal/IDE harnesses (`Claude Code`, `Codex`, `OpenCode`), operators can store durable organizational rules right beside their working files.
2. **Open-Standard `SKILL.md` Directories:** Rather than burying instructions inside one vendor's proprietary UI, teams package domain procedures into versioned folders (`SKILL.md` + `rules/` + `scripts/`).
3. **Protocol-Level `Elicitation` in MCP:** Under Linux Foundation governance, the Model Context Protocol (`MCP`) formalized `Elicitation`, allowing connected servers and tools to pause mid-execution and ask the human structured, typed clarifying questions before mutating state or generating high-stakes outputs.

## Anatomy of a Productive-Friction Skill

Compare how an ad-hoc prompt behaves versus an Interview-First `SKILL.md` across four departments:

| Department | Naive Ad-Hoc Prompt | Productive-Friction Interview Gate (Turn 1) | Reliability Gain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal** | *"Check this vendor MSA."* | Asks counterparty role, annual contract value, Green/Amber/Red data tier, and whether standard 2x liability cap applies before citing verbatim clauses. | Eliminates invented clause numbers and catches uncapped data-breach exposure. |
| **Finance** | *"Summarize Q3 SaaS variance."* | Confirms cash vs. amortized recognition rule, SSO inactivity window, and executes deterministic Python/SQL rather than naked token math. | Zero dropped CSV rows; cent-accurate reconciliation. |
| **Marketing** | *"Write a LinkedIn & Substack post on AI."* | Interviews the subject-matter expert for 1 real customer objection, 1 hard metric, and 1 contrarian trade-off before staging drafts. | Eliminates generic AI slop and platform demotion. |
| **Operations** | *"Document our onboarding SOP."* | Walks the senior operator through happy path + top 3 real-world failure exceptions in 20 minutes, compiling a reusable checklist and webhook spec. | Extracts tribal knowledge out of senior staff heads in one afternoon. |

## Turning Interviews Into Organizational Memory

The compounding payoff of Productive Friction goes beyond better single-turn outputs. Every time a Socratic intake skill interviews a senior operator, lawyer, or analyst, **have it save the confirmed edge-case rules into a versioned `rules/` file**. Within thirty days, your organization transitions from scattered ad-hoc chatting to an owned library of institutional judgment.
