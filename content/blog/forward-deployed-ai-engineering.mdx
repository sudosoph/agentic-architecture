Ask twenty mid-market CEOs or COOs why their 2025 AI initiatives stalled before reaching P&L impact and you hear two mirror-image stories:

1. **The $75,000 Slide Deck:** They hired a strategy consultancy that interviewed VPs for six weeks, delivered a matrix of "42 AI Use Cases," and left nobody capable of wiring an OAuth scope, an MCP server, or an evaluation harness.
2. **The Disconnected Prototype:** Internal or agency developers built a slick autonomous demo in isolation against clean synthetic data, handed it to Operations or Legal, and watched staff abandon it on Day 3 when real-world messy PDFs and unwritten exceptions broke every assumption.

## Why Forward Deployed AI Engineering Won

Originally pioneered by Palantir and now the defining enterprise delivery pattern across top 2026 AI leaders (`Harvey`, `Sierra`, and frontier lab deployment practices), **Forward Deployed AI Engineering (`FDE`)** puts a systems architect directly inside the business workflow alongside the operators who own the outcome.

Instead of separating "business discovery" from "production engineering," an FDE sprint runs one tight closed loop:

1. **Shadow Five Real Transactions Live (Days 1 to 2):** Never trust how an org chart describes a workflow. Watch an analyst, paralegal, or account manager process five real cases across tabs, spreadsheets, and emails. Use Socratic Productive-Friction interviews to surface the 10% edge cases that consume 80% of judgment.
2. **Ship a Thin Level-2 Slice Against Real Data (Days 3 to 5):** Connect canonical webhooks, layout-aware OCR or search APIs, and scoped `create-draft` outputs so operators inspect real staged work inside their actual tools within 72 hours.
3. **Lock In a 25-Case Binary Golden Eval Suite (Week 2):** Capture every operator edit from the first twenty-five runs, perform open-coding error analysis, and codify 3 to 5 binary `PASS`/`FAIL` checks (deterministic schema/citation verifiers plus a calibrated binary rubric judge) before expanding rollout.
4. **Harden Boundaries & Train Internal FDE Champions:** Lock down 4-legged OAuth scopes, disable silent public-cloud fallbacks on private `OpenCode` / `vLLM` setups, and train an internal champion in each department to maintain `SKILL.md` rules without permanent external dependency.

## Training Internal Forward Deployed Champions Inside Your Company

You do not need a fifty-person ML research group to run the FDE operating model. Some of the highest-leverage internal FDEs in 2026 are technical Product Managers, RevOps Architects, Financial Analysts, and Staff Engineers equipped with:
- Folder-native agentic harnesses (`Claude Cowork`, `Claude Code`, `OpenCode`)
- Standardized internal `MCP` tool servers
- A shared discipline around Productive-Friction `SKILL.md` specs and 25-case Golden Evals
