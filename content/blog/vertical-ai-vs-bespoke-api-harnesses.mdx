Every COO, CFO, General Counsel, and VP of Marketing faces the same procurement tension: dozens of vertical AI startups pitch per-seat subscriptions every quarter, while internal builders argue they can recreate any vendor demo over a weekend using frontier APIs and open weights.

Both extremes leak margin. Buying six disconnected department AI wrappers traps organizational context inside walled gardens that cannot share memory. Conversely, writing your own layout-aware PDF table parser, SOC2 Type II compliance envelope, or multi-jurisdiction legal corpus from scratch buries your team in undifferentiated plumbing.

## The three-lane decision framework: Buy, Build-With, or Compose

Instead of treating build-vs-buy as an ideology, classify every candidate workflow into one of three lanes:

1. **Buy Mature Vertical AI** when the vendor provides **proprietary external datasets, financial/payment rails, or deep regulatory risk transfer (`ISO 42001`, `SOC2 Type II`, `HIPAA BAA`)**. Examples include `Ramp` for corporate card controls and vendor price benchmarking, `Harvey` or `Legora` for global multi-jurisdiction legal practices, and `Glean` for enterprise-wide permissioned search across hundreds of connectors.
2. **Build On a Managed Agent OS** when operating high-concurrency external customer voice or chat experiences where sub-second streaming, telecom integration, and managed guardrails (`Sierra`-class platforms or cloud VPC gateways) let your team focus strictly on domain policy.
3. **Compose Lightweight In-House Harnesses** whenever a workflow encodes your **proprietary operating judgment**, bridges multiple internal systems (CRM + billing + inbox + docs), or replaces a thin per-seat SaaS wrapper with zero data moat.

## The six primitives behind clean in-house systems

Composing in-house does not mean reinventing infrastructure. High-leverage teams connect six battle-tested API layers:

- **Search & Live Web Grounding:** `Tavily`, `Exa`, and `Firecrawl` for cited Markdown extraction without maintaining brittle scraper fleets.
- **Layout-Aware Document & Table OCR:** `Reducto`, `Mistral OCR`, and structured vision schemas for multi-column invoices, contracts, and intake packets ([see our OCR benchmark](/blog/document-ocr-for-agents)).
- **Voice & Meeting Intelligence:** `Deepgram`, `Recall.ai`, and `Cartesia` feeding transcripts straight into your own CRM and follow-up queues rather than trapping notes in another per-seat silo.
- **Isolated Code & Browser Sandboxes:** `E2B` and `Cloudflare Sandboxes` so generated Python or SQL variance checks execute safely off host machines ([read why sandboxes matter](/blog/why-every-agent-needs-a-simulation-sandbox)).
- **Tiered Multi-Model Routing:** Sending eighty percent of high-volume classification and extraction to fast workhorse or open-weight endpoints while reserving deep reasoning models for complex synthesis ([full routing guide](/blog/gemini-4-neon-vs-deepseek-v4)).
- **Transactional Spine & Audited MCP:** `Postgres`, `Cal.com`, and [vetted MCP servers](/blog/mcp-server-audit-2026) paired with Draft-First human sign-off.

## Function-by-function verdict

| Department | Worth Buying Off the Shelf | Better Composed & Owned In-House |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing & Growth** | Primary search/SERP &amp; analytics datasets | Structured SME interview-to-essay workflows &amp; buyer-signal radars |
| **Finance & FP&A** | Spend rails &amp; corporate card platforms | Deterministic 3-way PO/invoice checks &amp; quarterly SaaS seat audits |
| **Legal & Contracts** | Global research suites at high matter volume | Verbatim-cited 25-clause NDA/MSA redline harness over ZDR endpoints |
| **RevOps & Delivery** | Core CRM system of record | Pre-call dossiers, post-call action extraction &amp; Draft-First follow-ups |
